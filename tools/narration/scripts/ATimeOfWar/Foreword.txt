[proudly] Dear reader, it is with great excitement that I offer this expansive work for your consideration: a recently unearthed, voluminous manuscript... an epic memoir, written over the course of three generations. It is a work as monumental and daunting as it is groundbreaking... a glimpse into the inner workings of the true scientific mind.

Upon discovery of these immaculate pages, I, suffering great personal expense, set forth to ensure the general publication and distribution of this important history. It is a work that will perhaps elucidate upon, and justify, the eccentric reputations of my forebears.

[with mock modesty] While I admit that I am biased in this appraisal, as it is my own exemplary lineage which is documented, I submit this comprehensive prosopography in as accurate a condition as I can muster. As one will soon attest, it is a chronicle of events of a decidedly fantastical nature... yet my research confirms it to be as true and vital a work as has ever been seen in print.

[grave] However, I must offer a word of caution to those with weaker constitutions. While these pages hold a brilliant catalogue of achievements, they also boldly depict the setbacks encountered along the journey. Gruesome, grim, and horrifying as they may be, I have included their spine-chilling contents as originally written. As one might expect, in exposing the base components of the universe, we uncover both light and darkness in equal measure. [quietly] For without darkness, we cannot appreciate the brilliance of the light.
